## Current Research View

**Neutral / wait for confirmation**。

NKE 是全球运动品牌龙头，但当前仍处在经营低谷和战略修复期。2026-07-08 旧报告后没有新的季度财报，关键变化是股价从 $43.21 继续跌至 2026-08-17 收盘 **$39.09**，接近/触及 52 周低位，股息率升至约 **4.2%**。价格更有吸引力，但 Direct、Greater China、Converse、终端 sell-through 与 FY2027 EPS 下修风险仍未解决。

## Current Main Thesis

- Nike、Jordan、Converse 仍拥有全球品牌资产、运动员资源和分销网络。
- FY2026 revenue **$46.4B**，reported flat，currency-neutral -2%；Q4 revenue **$11.0B**，reported -1%，currency-neutral -4%。
- Wholesale 正修复，但 Direct 仍弱：Q4 Wholesale **$6.6B**，reported +4% / currency-neutral +1%；NIKE Direct **$4.1B**，reported -7% / currency-neutral -9%。
- Q4 gross margin 49.2% 和 EPS $0.72 被 IEEPA tariff recovery 大幅抬高：gross margin 约 +900 bps benefit，EPS 约 +$0.52 benefit，不能年化。
- Greater China 和 Converse 是主要拖累项；FY2026 10-K 表示相关负面影响预计持续至 FY2027。
- 当前 $39.09 已进入可研究/小仓观察区，但强安全边际更接近 $28-$35。

## Key Risks

1. Brand heat and product innovation fail to recover.
2. FY2027 EPS falls below $1.50 and dividend payout pressure rises.
3. Greater China remains structurally weak.
4. NIKE Direct and digital channels keep declining.
5. Wholesale recovery proves low quality / channel stuffing.
6. Inventory cleanup requires promotions and damages normalized gross margin.
7. Converse reset takes longer than expected.
8. Investors overstate Q4 FY2026 EPS because tariff recovery is one-time.

## Core Thesis Assumptions That Must Remain True

- Nike brand remains culturally relevant globally.
- Product innovation in performance categories can restore consumer demand.
- Wholesale repair does not become channel stuffing.
- Direct channel can stabilize without excessive promotions.
- Greater China decline is cyclical/competitive but not permanent brand impairment.
- EPS does not fall materially below $1.50 and can recover toward $2.50-$3.25 over 2-3 years.
- Balance sheet and FCF remain strong enough to protect dividends while reinvesting.

## Red-Line Conditions

Review/downgrade if any of the following occur:

- FY2027 EPS expectation falls below $1.50 without credible recovery plan.
- Dividend payout exceeds 100% of earnings for more than a short transition period.
- Greater China continues double-digit currency-neutral decline beyond FY2027.
- NIKE Direct digital revenue continues double-digit decline.
- Inventory rises while revenue declines.
- Gross margin excluding one-time tariff recovery deteriorates further.
- Management relies on vague brand language without segment-level progress.

## Management Promises / Strategic Milestones to Track

- Sport Offense execution by sport category.
- Product portfolio rebalancing and reduction of excess supply in selected footwear products.
- Repositioning Nike Brand Digital as a full-price platform.
- Reinvestment in wholesale distribution and elevated physical retail presentation.
- Increased demand creation / brand marketing effectiveness.
- Cost realignment across supply chain and technology.

## What Would Change the Research View

- **Upgrade to Constructive**: Price remains near $35-$43 and FY2027 Q1/Q2 data show Direct, China, inventory and normalized gross margin stabilizing.
- **Upgrade to Positive**: Price falls below $35 without permanent brand impairment, or EPS recovery toward $2.50 becomes strongly supported by filings and guidance.
- **Downgrade to Negative**: EPS falls below $1.50, Direct/China keep double-digit declines, or dividend safety weakens materially.
