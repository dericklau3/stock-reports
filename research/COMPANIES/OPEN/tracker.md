# Opendoor Technologies（OPEN）Tracker

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current Research View

**Neutral / wait for confirmation（中性，等待扭亏和资本效率验证）**，中等偏低置信度。

OPEN 是美国最大 iBuyer 和住宅交易电商化代表公司。Q2 2026 的 acquisition contracts、homes purchased、contribution margin 和库存健康度改善，说明 turnaround 有真实进展；但公司仍 GAAP 大幅亏损、SBC 极高、股数一年增加约 23%、净债务约 $1.06B，商业模式仍高度暴露于房价、抵押贷款利率、库存周转和融资市场。

当前 $3.49 更像高 beta turnaround option，不是高确定性长期复利股。

## Current Main Thesis

- OPEN 的核心业务是 iBuyer：用 AI/数据定价买入房屋，维修和短期持有后转售，赚取 spread 和相关服务收入。
- 用户痛点真实：传统卖房流程慢、不确定、需要看房/谈判/维修/等贷款；OPEN 提供速度和确定性。
- Q2 2026 operational turnaround 信号明确：homes purchased 4,378，同比 +149%；contribution margin 5.8%；>120 days inventory 比例从 36% 降至 9%。
- 管理层目标：2026 年底按 12-month go-forward 口径实现 Adjusted Net Income positive。
- 但公司还没有证明 GAAP/FCF 盈利：Q2 2026 net loss -$162M，Adjusted EBITDA -$4M，Adjusted Net Loss -$30M。
- SBC/稀释是关键风险：H1 2026 stock-based compensation $244M，shares outstanding 约 971M，一年增加约 23%。

## Key Risks

- 房价下跌、交易量低迷或 mortgage rates 高企会导致库存周转慢、减值和 margin 压缩。
- iBuyer 模式资本密集，需要 non-recourse asset-backed debt 和其他融资支持库存。
- AI 定价模型在正常市场有用，但在快速变化市场中可能集中犯错。
- GAAP 亏损和 SBC 高，调整后盈利可能无法转化为每股价值。
- 当前估值不是深度安全边际：$3.49 对应市值约 $3.39B、EV 约 $4.46B、EV/Sales TTM 约 1.37x。
- 高 beta 3.59、short interest 约 17.3%，股价可能被情绪和 squeeze 主导。

## Core Thesis Assumptions That Must Remain True

1. Q3/Q4 acquisition cohorts can resell with contribution margin above 4%-5%.
2. 2026 year-end go-forward ANI positive is achieved or nearly achieved.
3. SBC declines materially from H1 2026 levels.
4. Homes purchased growth does not recreate aged inventory risk.
5. Mortgage rates and housing transaction volume stabilize rather than deteriorate.
6. Cash Now, More Later and related products reduce capital intensity without sacrificing customer value.

## Red-Line Conditions

- Q3 contribution margin falls below 4% or contribution profit does not improve as guided.
- Homes in inventory rise while >120-day inventory ratio increases.
- Cash balance declines materially or asset-backed financing availability tightens.
- SBC remains near Q2/H1 levels while adjusted profitability improves only on paper.
- Shares outstanding continue rising at double-digit annual pace.
- Net loss/operating cash burn worsens as acquisitions grow.

## What Would Change the Research View

### Upgrade

- Two consecutive quarters show high acquisition volume, healthy inventory, CM >4.5%, Adjusted EBITDA/ANI near or above breakeven, and SBC falling materially.
- Cash Now, More Later proves lower-capital, positive-margin growth.
- Housing market stabilizes while OPEN gains share without rebuilding aged inventory.
- Price remains below $3 while execution improves.

### Downgrade

- Q3/Q4 guideposts missed.
- Inventory aging returns or contribution margin compresses.
- Cash/debt position worsens as growth resumes.
- SBC and share dilution continue offsetting adjusted profitability.
- Management reframes the ANI-positive target or delays profitability.
