## Current Main Thesis

Nokia 的投资逻辑从旧报告的“中性 / 等待确认”上调到 **建设性但需继续验证**。核心变化是 Q2 2026 进一步证明 AI & Cloud / Optical / IP 网络需求真实增强，但 reported profit 和 FCF 尚未跟上。

核心判断：

- Q2 2026 net sales **€4.815B**，reported +8%，constant currency +9%。
- Q2 comparable operating profit **€434M**，同比 +18%；comparable operating margin **9.0%**，同比 +70 bps。
- Q2 reported operating loss **-€50M**，reported operating margin **-1.0%**，主要因 **€390M restructuring charges**。
- Q2 AI & Cloud order intake **€2.8B**，sales more than doubled year-on-year；管理层称约一半 orders 预计未来 12 个月转 revenue。
- Network Infrastructure Q2 net sales **€2.037B**，同比 +12%。
- Optical Networks Q2 net sales **€868M**，同比 +19%，constant currency +20%。
- IP Networks Q2 net sales **€679M**，同比 +15%，constant currency +16%。
- Q2 FCF **-€732M**；net cash and interest-bearing financial investments 环比减少 **€1.012B** 至 **€2.776B**。
- 2026 comparable operating profit outlook technically revised to **€2.1-2.6B** due to discontinued operations presentation; operationally outlook unchanged。

一句话：

> NOK 的 AI/cloud networking thesis 比 6 月更可信，但投资结论仍取决于 orders -> revenue -> reported profit -> FCF 的转化。

## Key Risks

- Q2 AI & Cloud orders 可能集中或前置，需要 Q3/Q4 验证。
- Orders 不等于 revenue；revenue 不等于 high-margin profit；profit 不等于 FCF。
- Q2 FCF **-€732M**，working capital outflow 约 **€1.15B**。
- Reported 与 comparable operating profit 差距大；Q2 restructuring charges **€390M**。
- Infinera integration 仍带来摊销、整合成本和产品路线风险。
- 美国 optical manufacturing capacity 扩张增加 capex、供应链和执行风险。
- Mobile Infrastructure 仍受运营商 capex 周期影响。
- AI-RAN platform 是长期选择权，短期不能按成熟收入池估值。
- 竞争强：Ciena、Cisco/Acacia、Arista、Huawei、Ericsson、Samsung 等。
- ADR/USD/EUR/IFRS/non-IFRS 口径复杂，估值需持续核对。

## What Would Change the Research View

Upgrade to **Positive** if:

- Q3/Q4 AI & Cloud orders remain strong;
- Q2 orders visibly convert to revenue;
- Optical/IP margins expand;
- reported operating profit turns positive and converges toward comparable;
- FCF turns positive;
- 2026/2027 guidance is raised;
- stock remains in the $8-$11 zone or lower.

Maintain **Constructive but watchful** if:

- AI/cloud orders and comparable profit stay strong;
- but FCF, reported profit, integration cost, and working capital remain noisy.

Downgrade to **Neutral / Negative** if:

- AI & Cloud orders sharply fall after Q2;
- revenue conversion is slower than management expectation;
- Q3 FCF remains materially negative;
- reported loss continues;
- Optical/IP growth proves low-margin;
- Infinera integration or manufacturing expansion costs exceed expectations.
