## Current Research View

**Constructive but valuation-constrained（基本面建设性，但估值强约束）**。

LITE 的 FY2026 Q4 和 Q1 FY2027 指引显著强于 2026-07-17 旧报告时的预期：Q4 revenue **$1.006B**、non-GAAP gross margin **50.4%**、non-GAAP operating margin **36.6%**、non-GAAP EPS **$3.23**；Q1 FY2027 revenue guide **$1.225B-$1.275B**、non-GAAP operating margin **39.5%-40.5%**、non-GAAP EPS **$4.05-$4.35**。AI 数据中心光互连、1.6T transceivers、OCS、CPO lasers 和 scale-across components 的需求是真实的。

但股价也已从旧报告锚点 **$706.23** 上升到 2026-08-19 收盘 **$827.60**。当前估值不是“便宜硬件股”，而是要求连续 beat-and-raise、订单转收入、毛利率维持和 FCF 转化。

## Current Main Thesis

- LITE 是 AI 光互连链条中受益很直接的 photonics/optical-interconnect 公司。
- FY2026 revenue **$3.014B**，同比 **+83.2%**；Q4 revenue **$1.006B**，同比 **+109.3%**。
- Components 和 Systems 同时高增长：FY2026 Components **$2.006B**，同比 **+79.7%**；Systems **$1.008B**，同比 **+90.7%**。
- Q1 FY2027 guide midpoint revenue **$1.25B**，说明增长没有在 Q4 结束。
- 估值已高度预支 FY2027/FY2028 bull case：EV/TTM sales **24.3x**，EV/Q1 FY2027 guide annualized sales **14.6x**。
- 资本结构复杂：所有 convertible notes 在 FY2027 Q1 可由持有人选择转换；截至 2026-08-14 已收到 **$757.8M** 本金的 early conversion requests。

## AI Optical Product Milestones to Track

- 100G and 200G EML shipments set new company records in Q4.
- 200G EMLs comprised **>25%** of total EML revenue.
- 200G lane-speed CW laser sales expanded across multiple transceiver customers.
- Ultra-high-power laser shipments for CPO solutions grew; company says meaningful revenue exiting CY2026 is on track.
- Narrow linewidth lasers achieved 10th consecutive quarter of sequential growth, up **>130% YoY**.
- Pump lasers grew **80% YoY**, with multiple long-term supply agreements for scale-across deployments.
- Systems revenue led by record cloud transceiver shipments.
- Initial 1.6T transceiver shipments began, with a portion using internal CW lasers.
- OCS ramp remains on track, backed by strengthening demand across a multi-year, multi-billion-dollar purchase agreement.

Important risk language from 10-K:

- Lumentum relies on a limited number of customers for a significant portion of sales.
- Majority of customers do not have contractual purchase commitments.
- Customers may alter purchasing behavior with little or no notice.
- Backlog is not necessarily indicative of actual revenue or orders for future periods because of delivery schedule changes and possible cancellations.

## Core Thesis Assumptions That Must Remain True

1. AI optical demand continues expanding through FY2027 and beyond.
2. Q1 FY2027 guide is not a near-term peak.
3. Components maintain strong growth and high margin.
4. Systems, especially cloud transceivers and OCS, keep scaling.
5. CPO lasers become meaningful revenue exiting CY2026.
6. Customer concentration does not turn into order volatility.
7. FCF conversion improves as revenue scales.
8. Convertible note settlement and share count remain manageable.

## Red-Line Conditions

- Q1 FY2027 misses or Q2 guide slows materially.
- Non-GAAP gross margin falls back below mid-40s without clear temporary reason.
- OCS agreement conversion timing slips or revenue contribution disappoints.
- CPO laser meaningful revenue milestone slips beyond CY2026.
- Customer A/B order pattern weakens materially.
- Diluted shares rise faster than revenue and FCF per share.
- FY2027 FCF remains weak despite high non-GAAP EPS.
- Management starts relying on TAM language without quantified order/revenue evidence.
