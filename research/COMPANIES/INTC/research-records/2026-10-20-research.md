## 可复算条件情景（研究日现值、非2028年价）
- t: 2026-10-20 through 2028-12-27 = 2.18754278 years (Actual days /365.25); quote used 2026-10-19 close.
- Conditional current EV equity-bridge claim in **$bn**: debt50.537 + NCI15.601 + leases0.473 − cash29.727 − assumed base-offer net19.7 = **17.184**. NET OFFER CASH ASSUMPTION NOT FINAL RECEIPT VERIFIED. Lease from FY25 only, not full updated period. Cash and debt start June27 but after issuance estimated; the bridge must be revalidated.
- Formula: present common equity per share = [ FY2028 revenue($bn) × normalized EBIT margin after SBC × EV/EBIT multiple ÷ (1+ equity_proxy_discount_rate)^2.18754278 − 17.184($bn) ] / scenario forecast economic shares($bn). This discounts future enterprise terminal value and subtracts current equity bridge as an approximation; it is a stress framework, not full DCF, and uses a shareholder rate proxy to discount EV so WACC/equity coherence is imperfect. **A stricter EV model should use scenario-specific WACC and debt/cash at aligned valuation dates**; therefore these numbers are model illustration rather than a validated intrinsic DCF.
- Bear: rev60, margin.10, FY28 EBIT6, exit15-20x, discount15%, shares5.5bn: **$8.9288–12.9465** vs92.03 = -90.30% to -85.93%.
- Base: rev75, margin.18, EBIT13.5, exit20-25x, discount13%, shares5.65: **$33.5352–42.6794** vs92.03 = -63.56% to -53.63%.
- Bull: rev90, margin.25, EBIT22.5, exit25-30x, discount12%, shares5.8: **$72.7254–87.8630** vs92.03 = -20.98% to -4.53%.
- Baseline price fall: 92.03/104.70 − 1 = **−12.1012%**.
- Economic stock marketcap proxy= 5.429105262bn × $92.03= **$499.6406bn**, P/S proxy 499.6406/57.032 ≈ 8.760x. Q2 2026 FY2026 NTM EPS current consensus & historical rolling daily forwardPE series not verified; negative GAAP makes P/E not helpful and avoids pretending to know full-year high/low.
- Reverse at $92.03 and bull top multiplier30x, bull share5.8bn, discount12%: implied2028 EBIT = (92.03×5.8+17.184)×1.12^2.18754278/30 = **$23.5323bn**; at rev90bn requires 26.147% after-SBC EBIT margin vs bullish 25% assumption. Sensitivity to cap table: each +0.1bn shares in bull terminal denominator depresses bull value at fixed value by around $1.2–1.5; additional $5bn unmodeled claim reduces value $0.86–0.91 per share. Each +1% EBIT margin at rev90bn, 30x, 12% discount changes bull high endpoint by around $4.0/share; combined inputs highly correlated and cannot cherry-pick favorable endpoints.
- Main known valuation fault: discounting future EV at an equity-like rate while bridge current debt may be conceptually inconsistent; treat **only a bounded stress-test demonstration**, not precise current fair value. Historical market multiple cannot substantiate these exit multiples. Until offer closing net funds, government warrants and lease changes, not possible to assert a certified present fair value.

## 经营进展和判定标准
- 18A: product Panther Lake announced Jan5, started available Jan2026; customer adoption of CPU products supports product business but not third-party foundry paying.
- 18A-P: risk production Jun2026, no scale-positive margin proof; monitor yields and reliable deliveries.
- High NA: Sep8 1m wafers includes process testing/early cert/R&D/select layers in limited Panther Lake models, not 1m independent foundry sellable wafers; monitor true productive utilisation/yield.
- 14A: Q2 10Q commitment to finish development, internal design plans, external significant customer still evaluating; company says expansion paced by committed demand. 2028 HVM goal from prior conference, not verified as customer contract; monitor binding demand and ROI.
- DCAI: Q2 $6.262bn growth+59% op$2.474bn; monitor Q3 order persistency, margins.
- External Foundry: Q2 $293m (vs $22m 2025), H1 $467m (vs $53m) out of total Foundry Q2 $5.765bn, gross and net ROI separately unverified; high growth on small base does not mean 18A leading edge profit.
- GPU D-Series Oct12 announced sampling; need large-scale deployment and gross margin.
- Google Apr9 multiyear product partnership, Fortinet Jul21 SP6 partner: both cooperation/possible paid customer paths but monetary minimum unknown, don't assign incremental revenue.
- Aug issuance: only price and exercise confirmed via examined disclosure; final receipt, economic shares and warrants require Q3 reconciliation.
- Failure/change tracking: $3.9bn goodwill impairment in Q1 Mobileye; historical 14A risk of being paused if insufficient demand, but 2026Q2 reaffirmed development. Don't hide downside, don't call delay without disclosed schedule comparison.
