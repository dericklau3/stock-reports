# AeroVironment（AVAV）Tracker

Last updated: 2026-09-11

## Current Research View

**Constructive but watchful / 结构性看好，但仍需要 SCDE 盈利、自由现金流和内控修复的进一步确认。**

与 2026-07-08 的研究相比，AVAV 的订单与 backlog 明显变强：Q1 FY27 bookings 约 **$683M**、book-to-bill **1.4x**，funded backlog 升至约 **$1.46B**；9 月又获得约 **$464.8M** 的 U.S. Army E-HEL / LOCUST 激光反无人机生产合同，并取得首个超过 **$50M** 的国际 LOCUST 订单。

但 BlueHalo 带来的 SCDE 业务仍然是主要不确定性：Q1 FY27 SCDE 收入同比下降约 **21%**，segment adjusted EBITDA 约 **-$8.9M**。因此当前观点仍维持 Constructive but watchful，而不是直接升级为 Positive。

## Current Main Thesis

AVAV 的长期逻辑是：

`无人机战争 + 精确打击 + 反无人机 + 定向能成为国防预算结构性重点 → AVAV 的 Switchblade、Puma、P550、JUMP 20、Titan、LOCUST 等平台获得持续订单 → funded backlog 转化为收入 → 产能扩张后利润率和 FCF 改善。`

公司已经从“小型无人机公司”升级为“自主系统 + 巡飞弹 + 反无人机 + 定向能 + 空天/网络任务系统”的国防科技平台。

## What Changed Since 2026-07-08

### Improved

1. **Funded backlog increased from about $1.2B to about $1.46B.**
2. **Q1 book-to-bill remained strong at 1.4x.**
3. **AxS confirmed itself as the profit engine**: revenue +21%, ~18% segment adjusted EBITDA margin.
4. **LOCUST materially strengthened the directed-energy thesis**: $464.8M U.S. Army E-HEL production award plus first >$50M international LOCUST order.
5. FY2027 revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance were maintained after Q1.

### Worsened / Still Unproven

1. SCDE revenue fell ~21% YoY and segment adjusted EBITDA remained negative.
2. FY27 FCF remains pressured by unusually high capex for capacity expansion.
3. Material weaknesses in internal controls remain unresolved.
4. BlueHalo's acquisition economics are still not proven through consolidated free cash flow.

## Practical Price Zones

- **Below ~$135**: attractive only if the operating thesis remains intact; would imply significant execution concerns are priced in.
- **$135-$170**: reasonable watchlist / research-entry zone.
- **$180-$225**: updated base-case fair-value zone.
- **Above ~$260**: requires bull-case LOCUST / SCDE execution and faster margin expansion.

## Core Assumptions That Must Remain True

1. AxS sustains double-digit revenue growth and high-teens segment EBITDA margins.
2. Switchblade / P550 / Puma / JUMP 20 demand remains durable.
3. LOCUST / counter-UAS awards convert into funded backlog and repeat production orders.
4. SCDE margins improve materially by FY28.
5. FY27 capacity capex improves FY28-FY30 revenue and free cash flow.
6. U.S. and allied defense budgets continue funding unmanned systems and counter-UAS.
7. No large new acquisition or dilution resets the integration timeline.

## Red-Line Conditions

Review or downgrade if:

- FY2027 revenue or adjusted EBITDA guidance is cut.
- Book-to-bill remains below 1.0 for multiple quarters.
- Funded backlog declines materially for multiple quarters.
- AxS growth slows to low single digits without a temporary timing explanation.
- SCDE remains structurally negative adjusted EBITDA into FY28 despite LOCUST awards.
- Free cash flow stays sharply negative after the capacity-expansion phase without corresponding backlog/revenue growth.
- Another major accounting restatement or material control failure occurs.

## Metrics to Monitor Next

- Funded backlog and book-to-bill.
- AxS revenue and segment adjusted EBITDA margin.
- SCDE revenue and segment adjusted EBITDA.
- LOCUST funded backlog and follow-on production orders.
- Product gross margin and mix.
- Operating cash flow, capex and FCF.

- Internal-control remediation status.

## What Would Make the View More Positive

- SCDE adjusted EBITDA turns positive and improves for at least two consecutive quarters.
- Funded backlog remains above ~$1.5B and book-to-bill stays >1.0.
- LOCUST receives additional funded production / international orders.
- FY28 FCF clearly inflects positive after the FY27 capex cycle.
- Material weaknesses are fully remediated.
- Revenue/EBITDA estimates rise without valuation re-expanding excessively.

## What Would Make the View More Negative

- FY2027 guide miss or cut.
- SCDE revenue decline continues while losses persist into FY28.
- Margin compression in AxS.
- Capex fails to produce revenue growth and cash conversion.
- Further goodwill impairment or acquisition-related surprises.
- Another significant accounting/control issue.
- Valuation moves above ~$260 without corresponding estimate upgrades.
